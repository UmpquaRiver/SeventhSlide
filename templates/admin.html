Active Profile

A style profile remembers the default themes for each output and the theme chosen on each song and announcement in the library. Switch profiles to flip the whole look at once — "New" starts blank (items fall back to the per-output default), "Duplicate" copies the current profile's assignments. Services and the items inside them keep their own themes and are never changed by switching profiles.
